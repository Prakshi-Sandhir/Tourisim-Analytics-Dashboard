# Tourism Performance Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a **Tourism Performance Insights Dashboard**, providing an interactive visual representation of tourism data. The dashboard showcases various key performance indicators (KPIs) and visualizations, offering insights into tourism patterns, revenue, and visitor demographics.

## Key Features

- **Visitors by Gender**: Displays the distribution of male, female, and other visitors.
- **Visitor by Region**: Highlights the regions visited the most, such as Kashmir, Goa, and others.
- **Travel Method**: Shows the preferred modes of travel (Road, Train, Helicopter, Cruise, etc.).
- **Yearly Variance**: Tracks visitor trends over the years (2018-2024).
- **Revenue Breakdown**:
  - Total Accommodation Revenue
  - Total Attractions Revenue
  - Total Food Revenue
  - Total Transport Revenue
- **Revenue by Nationality**: Analyzes revenue generated by tourists from various countries (Italy, Japan, Australia, etc.).
- **Top Region Visits vs. Revenue**: Compares region visits with corresponding revenue figures.
- **Purpose of Visit**: Segments visitors by their purpose of visit (Adventure, Business, etc.).
- **Average Stay Duration**: Highlights the average duration of visitor stays.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**: Used for creating visualizations and dashboards.
- **Data Sources**: Processed data related to tourism activities, regions, and revenue.


## Insights Derived

- Goa and Kashmir are top-performing regions in terms of visits and revenue.
- Adventure and business are significant purposes of visits.
- Revenue distribution varies significantly by nationality and travel method.
